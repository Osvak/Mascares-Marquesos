# Màscares i Marquesos: A VR Social Deduction Game at Palau Moja

**Màscares i Marquesos** (*Masks & Marquises*) is an online, multiplayer Virtual Reality (VR) social deduction game—inspired by *Among Us*—set within the historic 18th-century **Palau Moja** in Barcelona. 

Running natively on the **Meta Quest 2**, the game combines historical cultural heritage with competitive, role-based VR gameplay. Players navigate digitized rooms, complete maintenance and historical tasks, and work together to identify hidden impostors among the guests before the palace falls into chaos.

Developed within the **Giravolt Crea Lab** innovation laboratory, a joint initiative between **CITM-UPC** and the **Agència Catalana del Patrimoni Cultural** through its **Giravolt** program.

---

## 🎮 Gameplay Overview

- **Online Multiplayer & Social Deduction:** Designed as an *Among Us*-style social experience in VR. Players are assigned hidden roles (innocent guests/staff vs. saboteurs) and must use voice chat, deduction, and emergency meetings to discover who to vote out.
- **VR Mechanics on Meta Quest 2:** Runs natively on Quest 2 standalone headsets, utilizing VR controls for immersive task completion, spatial interaction, and spatial audio communication.
- **Palau Moja Setting:** Set inside photorealistic 3D environments of Barcelona's iconic Palau Moja, integrating digitized architectural spaces and cultural heritage assets.
- **Tasks & Sabotage:** Players explore the palace to complete mini-game tasks tied to palace upkeep and historical artifacts, while impostors trigger environmental sabotages and eliminate players unnoticed.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Hardware

- **Game Engine:** Unity
- **Target Hardware:** Meta Quest 2 (Native Standalone VR)
- **Networking:** Multiplayer Online Architecture
- **3D Assets & Photogrammetry:** Giravolt 3D Repository (Agència Catalana del Patrimoni Cultural)
